# 🌙 Salat Streak

Prayer times for **Québec City, Canada** — check off your 5 daily prayers and keep your streak alive.

## Features
- Live prayer times for Québec City (Fajr, Dhuhr, Asr, Maghrib, Isha) via the [Aladhan API](https://aladhan.com) — ISNA calculation method, America/Toronto timezone
- Next-prayer countdown, updated every second
- Gregorian + Hijri date
- Check off each prayer you prayed; progress bar for the day
- 🔥 **Streak**: consecutive days with all 5 prayers completed, plus best streak and total perfect days
- Browse previous days to see/fix check-ins, and a 28-day history grid
- Asr calculation setting: Shafi (default) or Hanafi
- Check-ins are saved in your browser only (`localStorage`) — no account, no server, nothing leaves your device
